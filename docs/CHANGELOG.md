## English

- The storage screen shows more rows of the grid: the title bar and the gaps between sections are smaller, and the inventory title only shows when the work area is collapsed or sits beside the inventory. With the work area open, the screen now fits completely in 1920×1080 full screen with the GUI scale on Auto, instead of cutting off the inventory.
- The scrollbar beside the storage grid is always shown. When there is nothing to scroll, its knob stays at the top, as does the Stonecutter recipe list's.

## 中文

- 存储界面能多显示几行存储格：标题栏和各区之间的间距变小，背包标题只在收起工作区或工作区与背包并排时显示。展开工作区时，1920×1080 全屏、界面尺寸为“自动”的情况下也能完整显示，不再截掉背包。
- 存储格右侧的滚动条始终显示；无需滚动时滑块停在顶端，切石机的配方列表也是如此。
